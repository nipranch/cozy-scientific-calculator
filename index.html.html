How to use Scientific Mode
  • Use sin, cos, tan for trigonometry.
  • Use sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹, tan⁻¹ for inverse functions.
  • DEG/RAD switches the angle unit.
  • xʸ calculates powers and x! calculates factorials.
  • √, log, ln handle common scientific functions.
  • π and e insert mathematical constants.
  • Use parentheses for grouped expressions, e.g. sin(30).
Tip: the calculator also supports your keyboard. Press Enter to calculate and Esc to clear.
